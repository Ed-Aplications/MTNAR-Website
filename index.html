Brakes, starters and more, fixed in your driveway

We'd rather be upfront about what we don't do, so nobody wastes their time.

Yes, we do

  • Brakes: pads, rotors and calipers, done at your home
  • Spark plugs and ignition coils
  • Starters, alternators and batteries
  • Suspension and steering: struts, control arms, tie rods
  • Sensors and check engine lights
  • Radiators, water pumps and cooling leaks
  • Window regulators
  • Not listed? Text us and we'll tell you yes or no.

Not for us

  • A/C work (for now)
  • Transmissions or clutches
  • Timing chains or belts
  • Head gaskets or engine replacement
  • Tires, alignments or oil changes
  • Same-day or emergency calls
  • Jobs that need the car apart over several visits

For those, a full shop with a lift is the better call, and we'll tell you so.

How booking works

  1. Text us

    Send the year, make and model and what's going on to 931-674-1516, or message us on Facebook. The short form below can write the text for you.

  2. Send a few details

    Your written estimate needs your full name, address, phone, email, VIN and mileage. The VIN gets the exact right part, and the address tells us how far we're driving. We use these only for your estimate and invoice, never shared.

  3. Approve your estimate

    You get a written, flat-rate estimate. Nothing starts until you approve it.

  4. Pick a time

    We usually book one to two weeks out. We're by appointment only.

  5. We fix it where it's parked

    We come to your driveway, do the job and clean up. If we find something else, we tell you and you decide.

Questions people ask us

Don't see yours? Text us and we'll answer.

Do I need to be home?

No. As long as we can get to the car and the keys, you don't need to be there. If we find anything else along the way, we'll text you and you decide.

How soon can you come out?

We usually book one to two weeks out. We're by appointment only, so we can't take same-day or emergency calls.

How do I pay?

Cash, card, check, Venmo, Google Pay, Apple Pay, or pay online from your invoice.

Can I buy my own parts?

Yes, we're happy to install parts you supply. Match them to your VIN when you order so the job doesn't stall on the wrong part. Parts you supply are installed at your own risk, as your written estimate explains.

Where can you work on my car?

Most driveways work fine. We need a flat, paved spot with room to work around the car. Some apartment complexes and HOAs don't allow car repairs, so check your rules first. If you're not sure, text us and we'll figure it out together.

What if the weather's bad?

All of our work is weather permitting, since we're working outside. If rain or storms move in, we'll text you and set a new time.

Do you come to my town?

We serve Spring Hill, Thompson's Station, Columbia and Franklin. Nearby? Text us your town and we'll let you know.

What if I need to reschedule?

Just text us at least 24 hours ahead and we'll find a new time at no charge.
