Bizline Privacy Policy

Last updated October 23, 2026

Bizline is an app for texting from your VoIP.ms numbers. It is not made by VoIP.ms and is not affiliated with or endorsed by VoIP.ms.

What the App Keeps on Your Phone

  • Your messages with each number, the pictures in them (in the app's private storage, under 500 MB), your drafts, and which conversations you archived (and when you last put one back).
  • Your VoIP.ms email, and your API password in your phone's secure storage (never in the app's log or settings).
  • Names from your contacts, only if you turn on Show Contact Names, and only for numbers you have a conversation with.
  • Your quick replies (your saved messages), in the app's database, so an export carries them.
  • Your settings, and an app log of what the app did (never message text, passwords, full phone numbers or names).

All of it stays in the app's private storage, which other apps can't read. The app doesn't encrypt its database itself: Android keeps it private to the app. None of it goes to Google's cloud backup or to a new phone by device transfer.

What the App Sends, and to Whom

  • Only to VoIP.ms (voip.ms): your email and API password with each request, to read your numbers and messages and to send the texts and pictures you send. Pictures are downloaded only from VoIP.ms.
  • Instant Alerts (only if you turn them on): your phone's push address from Google (Firebase Cloud Messaging) and a random code go to Bizline's alert service (bizline-push.edhensel3.workers.dev, run on Cloudflare), which keeps them until you turn Instant Alerts off. Each texting number's SMS URL Callback in VoIP.ms is set to that service's address with the code; when a text arrives, VoIP.ms sends it only the number that got the text, and the service sends your phone a push through Google saying a text arrived. The service never receives, keeps or logs the text, who sent it, your password or your contacts. Turning Instant Alerts off or signing out puts each number's callback back and deletes the code and push address from the service. The app keeps the code, your numbers' earlier callback addresses and which numbers it changed in its settings.
  • Crash reports (on unless you turn off Settings → Phone & Data → Privacy → Send Crash Reports): when the app crashes, Google's Firebase Crashlytics receives the kind of error and where in the app's code it happened, with the app version, phone model, Android version and a random install code Crashlytics makes. Never the error's own words, your messages, phone numbers, contacts, voicemails, call records, email or passwords. Google keeps crash reports for 90 days. Turned off, nothing is sent.
  • Nothing to us, to Anthropic, or to anyone else, except feedback you send yourself (below), crash reports (above) and Instant Alerts if you turn them on (above). The app has no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • Feedback you send (Settings → Send Feedback) goes to the Bizline team's Google Form (docs.google.com), only when you tap Send: what it's about, the part of the app, your message, your email if you give it, and, with Include App and Phone Details ticked, the app version, phone model, Android version, whether calling is set up and how many numbers you have. The page lists them before you send. Nothing else goes: no phone numbers, contacts, messages, voicemails, call records or passwords. A message not sent yet is kept in the app's settings until it's sent or you clear it.
  • A web link you tap in a message opens in your phone's browser or the app that owns it.
  • An export file goes only where you save it, with a password if you chose one.
  • Calls go only to VoIP.ms, encrypted (TLS and SRTP), through the app's own sub-account on your account (made by Set Up Calling). Its password is kept in your phone's secure storage (never the app's log or settings); VoIP.ms keeps it too, like any sub-account's. The app keeps no call history or recording; a call's audio goes only to VoIP.ms. The Calls tab reads your call records from VoIP.ms; it never asks for your phone's own call log. The Voicemails tab reads your voicemails from VoIP.ms. Both lists (the last 91 days of calls, and the voicemails: who called, when and how long) are also kept on the phone, so they show at once when you open the app; VoIP.ms's answer then replaces them. Call recordings, voicemails and their transcripts, and those two lists, are saved on the phone, in the app's private folder, so they open at once: never in Google's backup or a device transfer, never logged, removed when deleted in the app or at VoIP.ms, and all removed on Sign Out. What you delete on this phone only is remembered as those codes, so it isn't downloaded again (forgotten on Sign Out). File names are codes, never a number, a name or a time. What you archive is remembered on the phone as VoIP.ms's id for each call and a code for each voicemail: never a number, a name, a time or a message. Settings → VoIP.ms Settings keeps VoIP.ms's last answer in the app's settings (your numbers and what they're set to, the email addresses and forward numbers they use, your sub-accounts' names, the transcript email, your voicemail boxes' email and the last 30 days' charges and payments), so it opens at once. It never holds a password or a PIN, is never in Google's backup, and is removed on Sign Out.
  • The Calling Test (Debug & Testing only) connects the same way with a SIP username and password you type for the test; they are never saved.

Notifications

A notification shows who texted and the start of the text. On the lock screen, your phone's own setting decides whether the text shows. With Hide Message Text, notifications say only New message.

Permissions

Permission Why
Internet (INTERNET) Talking to VoIP.ms.
Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS) Telling you a text arrived. Asked first; the app works without it.
Contacts (READ_CONTACTS) Names instead of numbers. Asked first, only if you choose it; nothing is ever written to your contacts.
Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO) Your voice in a call. Asked before your first call.
MODIFY_AUDIO_SETTINGS, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_SPECIAL_USE Calls: putting the phone in call mode and sending call audio to the earpiece or speaker; and, in Debug & Testing's Calling Test, keeping a test registration up overnight when you turn on Stay Registered. Not asked; nothing personal.
FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MICROPHONE Keeps the microphone working during a call if the screen goes off or you leave the app (a Call in progress notification shows, with no number). Not asked; nothing personal.
VIBRATE, WAKE_LOCK, RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, FOREGROUND_SERVICE, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_SHORT_SERVICE Added by the notification and background-check parts: checking for texts on a timer, also after a restart, and only with a connection. The Calling Test also uses ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE to move a call when Wi-Fi and mobile data switch. Not asked; nothing personal.
com.google.android.c2dm.permission.RECEIVE Added by Google's push messaging: receiving Instant Alerts' wake-up. Not asked; nothing personal.

Camera, photos and files are chosen through Android's own camera app and pickers, so the app asks for no camera or storage permission.

How Long Things Are Kept, and Deleting Them

Messages stay on your phone until you delete them (Delete Conversation, Settings → Database → Delete All Messages) or sign out choosing Delete. Signing out with Delete removes the messages, pictures and password; the app log and settings stay until you uninstall the app or use Android's Clear Storage for it, which removes everything. VoIP.ms keeps its own copy of your messages under its own policy.
