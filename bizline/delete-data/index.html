Delete Your Bizline Data

Bizline (Android app) · Last updated October 21, 2026

How to ask

  1. Email info@middletnautorepair.com with the subject Bizline data deletion.
  2. Say which email address you used in Bizline's Send Feedback form (if you gave one), and roughly when you sent feedback.
  3. We delete it within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • Feedback you sent from Settings → Send Feedback: your message, the email you gave and the app and phone details sent with it. This is the only data Bizline's developer receives.

What we don't hold

Bizline has no accounts and no server of its own. Your messages, pictures, calls, voicemails, contacts and passwords are never sent to us, so there is nothing of them for us to delete.

  • On your phone: delete conversations in the app, use Settings → Database → Delete All Messages, sign out choosing Delete, or uninstall Bizline to remove everything it kept.
  • At VoIP.ms: your messages, call records, voicemails and recordings are kept by VoIP.ms under its own policy. Delete them in your VoIP.ms account, or ask VoIP.ms. Bizline is not affiliated with VoIP.ms.
