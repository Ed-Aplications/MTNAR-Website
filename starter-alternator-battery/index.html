Signs something's wrong

  • A click, or nothing at all, when you turn the key
  • Slow, labored cranking
  • A battery or charging light on the dash
  • Dim or flickering lights
  • A battery that keeps going dead

What we do

  • Starter replacement
  • Alternator replacement, with a charging system test after
  • Battery replacement and terminal cleaning
  • Loose, corroded or worn battery cables and connectors

Worth knowing

Check the simple stuff first. A no-start can be as simple as a loose or corroded battery terminal. We test before we replace parts, so you only pay for what's actually bad.

Before the first freeze: if your battery is 3 to 5 years old, cranks slow or has crusty terminals, get it tested now instead of on a cold morning. Most parts stores will test it for free.
